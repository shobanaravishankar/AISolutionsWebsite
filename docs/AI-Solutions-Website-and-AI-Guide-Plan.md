# AI Solutions Website and AI Guide

### Project charter and delivery plan

Version 1.0 | September 28, 2026 | Planning baseline

Prepared for the AI Solutions project owner and delivery team.

### Purpose

We want a website that draws people into our ideas, helps them understand what we are building, and gives them a welcoming way to participate. A friendly intelligent agent will answer questions using an approved FAQ and material from our GitHub documentation, including our constitution, ideas, and operating system concepts.

This document preserves the direction of the project and provides a detailed to-do list. It is the starting reference for later design and implementation. The immediate deliverable is this documented plan; the delivery sequence and technical choices below remain proposals for subsequent work.

### Requirements from the discussion

- Create an engaging website that makes visitors curious and invites them to become involved.

- Provide an intelligent conversational guide with a warm, friendly, smooth, charming, and welcoming manner.

- Ground answers in a strong FAQ and approved GitHub material about the constitution, ideas, and operating system vision.

- Open the guide from a glowing circular button at the bottom right that stays visible while the visitor scrolls.

- Explore purple with a sparkling gold electrical ring as the latest visual direction for the button.

- Invite visitors to follow the business. Include Facebook and Instagram; consider TikTok as an optional addition.

- Keep a durable written record and build on it so ideas survive transitions between conversations and contributors.

### Boundaries of this version

AI Solutions and JARVIS remain the existing project references; final public naming can be confirmed during planning. No launch date, budget, provider, social handle, or publication commitment is established by this document. All 80 checklist items begin as open planning tasks, including verification of any work that already exists.

## The website experience and glowing agent button

### The visitor journey

A visitor arrives, understands the purpose of the project, and explores the ideas at their own pace. The glowing button provides an obvious way to ask a question from anywhere on the page. The guide explains the relevant idea, offers a useful next step, and can invite the visitor to follow an official social account when that fits the conversation.

### Persistent circular launcher

The button stays in the bottom right of the visible browser window while the page scrolls. This records the scrolling behavior described in the discussion. It does not require the button to chase the pointer. Clicking or tapping it opens the conversation panel; closing the panel returns the visitor to the page.

| Element | Design requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Shape | A clearly circular, prominent launcher with an easy click or tap target. |
| Position | Bottom right of the viewport with enough spacing from edges and other controls. |
| Appearance | Purple with sparkling gold around the ring; an electrical energy effect. Exact balance to be refined in a visual review. |
| Motion | Gentle glow and flowing sparks. A quieter version for visitors who reduce animation. |
| Meaning | An accessible label such as Ask our AI guide, so appearance is not the only explanation. |
| States | Clear ready, connecting, responding, unavailable, open, and closed states. |

### Conversation panel

Clicking the circle should bring a live AI guide onto the screen for natural, responsive conversation. The panel should remain readable on a phone and provide a clear close button. Visitors can type naturally, select starter questions, and follow approved source links. Spoken conversation is part of the intended experience; whether voice ships with the first release remains an open delivery decision.

### Interaction quality

The guide introduces itself as an AI assistant, responds in plain language, and adapts the amount of detail to the question. It answers before suggesting a follow action, accepts a declined invitation, and avoids repeating promotions. Sound and microphone use begin only through an intentional visitor action.

The earlier blue, purple, red, black, white, and yellow mixture is preserved as an explored option. Purple with sparkling gold is the latest preferred direction, subject to seeing the design.

## The knowledge base and conversation standard

### From GitHub documents to useful answers

The project owner identified GitHub as the location of the constitution, ideas, and operating system material. The delivery team will identify the exact repositories and files, review which content is suitable for visitors, and turn that material into a readable FAQ and a source collection for the guide.

The proposed approach is to search the approved source collection for each question and give the guide the relevant passages. This lets answers refer back to the project material and makes updates easier to review. The implementation method remains open until the existing agent and website are assessed.

### Content rules

- Separate current capabilities, demonstrations, work in progress, and future ambitions in both the FAQ and generated answers.

- Preserve the meaning of the constitution. Public summaries should be reviewed by the project owner.

- Use only material selected for visitor answers. Access to a private repository does not make every file public content.

- Keep a source title, location, version or revision, and review date for each approved entry.

- When the sources do not answer a question, acknowledge the gap and offer a relevant page or contact route.

- Treat retrieved text as reference material. It must not change the agent rules or grant new permissions.

### Initial FAQ topics

| Topic | Questions to prepare |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose and people | What are we building? Who is it for? Why does it matter? |
| Constitution | What principles guide the project? How do they affect decisions? |
| Operating system ideas | What is the vision? How does AI fit? What would a person actually do with it? |
| Development status | What works today? What is a prototype? What is planned? |
| Trust and control | What can the guide access? What information is collected? How do people stay in control? |
| Participation | How can someone follow progress, ask questions, contribute, or contact the team? |

### A welcoming voice

The guide should sound thoughtful, friendly, and inviting without exaggerating its abilities. Smooth communication and quick responses are priorities. Natural dialogue is the intended standard, with clear AI identity. Response-time targets should be agreed and tested; a promise of zero failures would not be a useful acceptance criterion.

## Social channels and proposed delivery sequence

### Following the business

The main invitation is to follow the business and stay connected. The user refined an earlier mention of subscribing to following; this plan does not assume a paid subscription, mailing list, or registration wall. Those would be separate decisions if they become useful later.

| Channel | Scope recorded | Proposed first connection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | Required presence | Verified official profile or page link, clear branding, and a link back to the website. |
| Instagram | Required presence | Verified official account link, consistent branding, and a link back to the website. |
| TikTok | Optional | Assess audience fit and content capacity, then add an official account if selected. |

### Define the depth of integration

A first release can provide useful follow links and context-aware invitations from the guide. Live feeds, account publishing, direct-message replies, and automated cross-posting are deeper integrations. Each needs a defined purpose, account access, an operating owner, and verification of the platform capabilities available at implementation time.

Do not count an outbound click as a completed follow unless the platform provides reliable confirmation. Public posts and replies should follow an agreed review process; the current task creates the planning document only.

| 5  Validate | Evidence that answers, mobile use, failure handling, and links work. | Integrated preview. |

## Acceptance criteria and measures

These checks define the proposed evidence for a first public release. The delivery team should agree numerical targets during discovery rather than imply a service commitment before the runtime is assessed.

| Area | Evidence required |
| --- | --- |
| Project clarity | A first-time visitor can explain the purpose and distinguish current work from future plans. |
| Circular launcher | The circle remains visible at the bottom right during scrolling and opens and closes the guide on phone and desktop. |
| Visual direction | The owner has reviewed the purple and sparkling gold design, including its quieter motion setting. |
| Knowledge quality | Reviewed test answers are supported by approved sources; gaps and conflicting information are handled honestly. |
| Conversation | The guide is friendly and useful, answers follow-ups, and avoids repetitive or pressuring invitations. |
| Live connection | A real end-to-end conversation works through the chosen runtime, and failures are visible and recoverable. |
| Social channels | Official Facebook and Instagram links resolve correctly. TikTok appears only if selected and ready. |
| Voice if selected | Listening, speaking, stopping, interruption, and the text alternative work in realistic conditions. |
| Privacy and control | Data handling is explained, conversations remain separate, and secrets or private documents are not exposed. |
| Operational handoff | Owners, update procedures, spending visibility, and a recovery route are documented. |

### Measures to collect

Review answer usefulness, factual accuracy, source coverage, unresolved-question rate, response delay, failed requests, and cost per useful conversation. Track launcher opens and outbound social clicks where appropriate. Review platform follower totals separately; a link click alone does not prove that someone followed.

### Release decision

The project owner reviews the integrated experience and outstanding issues. Delivery should distinguish finished features from deferred ideas and name the person responsible for support. Any serious source-accuracy, privacy, or basic usability failure should be resolved before public release.
